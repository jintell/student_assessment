## Required Outbox Checks

Apply to each target deployment after a representative synthetic workload drains. Record the observation window with Ops; an instantaneous empty sample is insufficient. All checks remain open until fresh live evidence is attached.

- [ ] **Backlog at zero:** PENDING and CLAIMED rows are zero after drain, oldest pending age is zero, and no expired claims remain. Cross-check fresh state gauges with approved metadata queries. PUBLISHED retained history is not backlog. NaN, absent or stale values fail the check.
- [ ] **No FAILED rows:** fresh database/metric count is zero; all earlier failures have classified causes and authorized audited recovery evidence. Never delete rows to satisfy the check.
- [ ] **DLQs empty:** `integration.dlq` and applicable consumer dead-letter queues have zero ready and unacknowledged messages, no new ingress during the window, and resolved dispositions. Any unhandled-version incident has compatible consumer deployment proof, not blind replay.
- [ ] **Relay ticking:** deployed worker assembly and enabled `outbox-relay` registry reservation match. Owner success/last-success evidence and completed `outbox relay batch` spans advance; a synthetic event publishes and its guarded consumer completes. Merely increasing contender/attempt tick counts is insufficient. Confirm no competing owner and demonstrate expired-claim recovery using the actual configured reclamation cadence.

## Attach Before Sign-Off

Retain environment/cluster/namespace, immutable release image, deployment revisions, UTC window, registry/configuration snapshot, scrape freshness and dashboard links, metadata counts, broker ready/unacknowledged counts, owner-success and batch-span evidence, and a restricted synthetic event/correlation/trace reference. Keep payloads and secrets out of the evidence bundle. Include operator audit references for every recovery action.

Attach the P9.2 staged backlog threshold/routing/recovery proof, adopted alert/dashboard decisions and operator runbook review. Baseline backlog thresholds and the four additional alert proposals are distinct; repository proposals are not installed alerts.
