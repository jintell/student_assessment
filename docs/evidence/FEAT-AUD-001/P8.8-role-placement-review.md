   with FEAT-PLAT-006; supply real KMS, signature, database, evidence and policy
   dependencies. Choose an approved stable job actor and narrow module boundary.

   job, the other does no sealing/verification work; observe release/failover
   and shutdown, then verify dense root sequences and unique immutable seals.
   Keep direct competing-sealer CAS tests as a separate integrity control.
4. Start real api, worker and pindist contexts and prove only the intended
   background assembly has these jobs; verify its signing privilege boundary.
