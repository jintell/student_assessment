# P8.7 post-restore full re-walk — FEAT-OPS-003

Repository handover published 2026-10-17. FEAT-AUD-001 owns verification and
evidence preservation; FEAT-OPS-003 owns restore orchestration and its release
decision. This publishes the existing hook, not a completed restore drill,
deployed command or recipient acknowledgement.

## Hook and required wiring

The existing internal hook is
`AuditFullVerifier.verify(AuditFullVerifier.Trigger.POST_RESTORE)`, returning
`Mono<FullVerificationSummary>` with trigger, retained-chain count and tenant
root-chain count. It re-walks retained chain records/checkpoints and verifies
all supplied tenant roots and signatures. The daily verifier's open-chain-only
work bound is not sufficient after restore.

Use a reviewed module/operations adapter that invokes the hook within audit's
closed module; do not import its application/domain internals from an ops
feature or expose it as an ordinary request endpoint. Supply real read-scoped
evidence adapters, all historical codec/public-key versions, signature
verification, finding capture and the preservation/alert/halt implementation.
`R2dbcAuditFullVerificationEvidence` is the existing database evidence adapter;
its coverage must match the restore inventory, including retained seals and
checkpoints for disposed epochs. Neither a fake signature verifier nor a
partial tenant inventory is acceptable restore evidence.

## Restore sequence and result

1. Restore the approved database/evidence artifacts and record immutable
   restore identity, source/target, schema/codec/key versions and expected
   tenant/chain inventory. Keep normal writes, sealing and disposition gated
   until verification and the owning recovery criteria succeed.
2. Invoke the full verifier once through the background operations boundary
   with POST_RESTORE; await terminal completion and retain its result. Do not
   fire-and-forget or block an event-loop thread.
3. Compare reported coverage with the expected restore inventory. An empty or
   incomplete evidence provider can complete with zero counts; successful
   completion alone must not certify an unexpectedly empty restore.
4. Retain the trigger, sanitized restore/run identity, times, expected/observed
   counts, outcome and immutable findings references in the recovery evidence
   register. Only the owning restore workflow may release its gate after all
   recovery criteria pass.

On `AuditVerificationMismatch`, the failure handler preserves evidence,
records a high-severity metric, raises P1 and halts tenant sealing/disposition,
then propagates the mismatch. Never repair hashes, rewrite checkpoints, choose
a preferred fork or re-sign old evidence. An unavailable key/database or
cancelled/incomplete run is not a pass; preserve the operational failure and
keep restore acceptance blocked even when it is not classified as tampering.
