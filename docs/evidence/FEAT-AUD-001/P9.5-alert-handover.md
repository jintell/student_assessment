The [P9.1 catalogue](../../metric-catalogue.md) now registers the integrity
adapter, but live producers/freshness, lock-wait/skew exemplars, P1 sinks and
scheduler assembly remain blocked under P9.2–P9.4/P8.8. Missing/NaN/stale
observations mean unknown, not healthy. Counter resets on process restart must
not erase the durable incident record. Scope queries to the target deployment
and do not sum duplicated cluster-wide snapshots.

Operations must resolve the unspecified duration of "sustained" for CAS with
the audit owner, preserving the >5/min and >10 thresholds. A bounded aggregate
must not hide a single starving tenant; protected per-tenant evidence is
required without identity labels. Confirm actual retry semantics: the current
sealer's default bounded retries can defer repeatedly across invocations, so
one process counter alone is not an epoch-level retry history.

Closure requires controlled positive/negative exercises of all seven rules,
P1/P2/P3 routing evidence, missing-data behavior, and links to the P9.7–P9.11
runbooks. No FEAT-OPS-004/L5 or live-alert acceptance is closed by this handover.
