# P8.4 disposition and hold protocol — FEAT-PRIV-001

Repository handover published 2026-10-17. FEAT-AUD-001 owns chain verification,
immutable evidence, ordered execution and persistence adapters. FEAT-PRIV-001
owns policy approvals/versions, partition-wide eligibility, legal holds,
authorization, scheduling, and approved archive/drop execution. Both own
`ARC-VERIFY-032` evidence: audit supplies the integration proof and privacy
supplies policy/hold execution and the production lifecycle. A7 remains a
Phase 6 gate; this handover is not consumer implementation or sign-off.

## Required invocation boundary

The current internal implementation is `AuditDispositionExecutor` with
`DispositionRequest` and `AuditDispositionEligibility`; holds use
`AuditHoldService`. A request includes request ID, tenant, class/month epoch,
policy key/version, original retention start, due time and authorization
reference. The `audit` module is closed; these application-package types are
not a published Modulith named interface. Consumer integration must expose an
approved narrow module API or event boundary, not import internal adapters.

Install the approved retention identity for DDL. Query/emission and hold state
must use their secured caller-owned transaction; never give ordinary request
roles detach/drop capability. Evaluate eligibility over the entire physical
class/month leaf: it may contain several tenants, so one tenant's due date or
hold list cannot authorize partition-wide removal. The present tenant-scoped
integration fixture is not proof of that production-wide orchestration.

## Mandatory order

1. Freeze the target and verify shard links, sequences, checkpoints, counts
   and heads against consistent evidence.
2. Reproduce the root, verify the unique signed seal and key version, and
   establish its membership in the dense tenant root chain. An unsealed epoch
   cannot proceed.
3. Commit and verify `audit.AUDIT_EPOCH_DISPOSED.v1` in a current open epoch.
   Include epoch, sequence ranges, root hash, seal/signature reference,
   `policy_key`, version, request and authorization references.
4. Recheck holds, due time and longer/newer policy immediately before detaching
   the exact leaf. Archive/drop requires the applicable policy and identity.
5. Re-verify retained epoch tails and the root chain using retained permanent
   seals/checkpoints. Only then mark the request complete.

No step may be skipped. Preserve evidence and escalate verification failure;
never repair a chain or select a preferred fork. A concurrent seal must remain
observable and valid through the disposition. Persist progress and deduplicate
requests. The current executor always starts at step one: production recovery
after a committed detach still needs the owning workflow's state-aware resume
path. Do not blindly replay the whole executor after partial DDL completion.

## Legal hold

Any active intersecting hold suspends the whole leaf as `HOLD_SUSPENDED` and
emits `audit.AUDIT_EPOCH_DISPOSITION_SUPPRESSED.v1` with opaque hold/legal-basis
references, policy version, original clock and correlation. The same suspension
is idempotent. Do not split/repartition held evidence or copy legal narrative
into audit payloads. Every applicable hold must be inactive before audited
release (`audit.AUDIT_EPOCH_HOLD_RELEASED.v1`). Resume policy evaluation with
the stored original start/due times; release never resets the retention clock.
A 90-day P3 review does not authorize release or disposal.
