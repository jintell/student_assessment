# P8.2 retention interface — FEAT-PRIV-001

Repository handover published 2026-10-17. Producer: FEAT-AUD-001; consumer and
policy-version-store owner: FEAT-PRIV-001. No recipient acknowledgement or
production policy service is asserted.

The public contract is `org.meldtech.platform.shared.kernel.audit`:
`RetentionClass`, `RetentionPolicyView`, `RetentionDecision` and
`RetentionHorizon`. Consumers depend on these inward contracts, not
`audit.domain.RetentionResolver`. The approved minimums are recorded in the
[retention-class card](phase-1-discovery.md#p12-retention-class-card):

| Enum value | Retention obligation | Physical class partition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RESULT_CORRECTION_EVIDENCE | At least five years or the associated result lifetime, whichever is longer | audit_event_p_result_correction |
| RESULT_PUBLICATION_EVIDENCE | At least five years or the associated result lifetime, whichever is longer | audit_event_p_result_publication |
| PIN_SECURITY_EVENT | 12 months | audit_event_p_pin_security |
| GENERAL_AUDIT_EVENT | Two years | audit_event_p_general |

These obligations are policy inputs, not durations hard-coded into the enum.
Class partitions contain UTC calendar-month leaves; shard IDs are columns.
Legal hold suspends the whole covered class/month unit regardless of its due
date. Result-lifetime and hold evidence must not be inferred from an enum name.

`RetentionPolicyView.resolve(occurredAt, eventType, entityType, candidates)` is
synchronous. Supply an approved, immutable snapshot without database/network
I/O on the request thread. Return a nonblank policy key, positive append-only
version, effective interval `[effectiveFrom, effectiveUntil)`, horizons for
every candidate and the winning class. An absent end is unbounded; an absent
retained-until value means indefinite retention, not immediate expiry.

The emitter independently checks effective-time coverage and candidate
completeness, then selects the longest horizon. Indefinite outranks dated;
equal horizons use correction, publication, general, then PIN precedence.
It rejects an inconsistent winner. Placement and policy key/version are
captured at write time before canonical hashing. Queries do not reclassify
stored rows. A failed decision fails the enclosing business transaction.

Policy approval, version persistence, snapshot refresh, result-lifetime
resolution and disposition scheduling belong to FEAT-PRIV-001. A longer/new
classification is represented by new audit evidence referencing the original;
never UPDATE or move existing evidence. The P2.11 proposed
`privacy.RETENTION_RECLASSIFIED.v1` still requires the owning feature's reviewed
catalogue entry and workflow. Later disposition must consider that evidence.

Validation: existing `RetentionResolverTest` passes (indefinite longest-wins
and inconsistent-winner refusal). P7.16 additionally retains real PostgreSQL
placement, effective-version-boundary and immutable reclassification checks in
[the integration report](phase-7-scope-disposition-testing.md).
