# P8.6 audit alerts and dashboard 7 — FEAT-OPS-004

Repository handover published 2026-10-17. Source: approved architecture
§16.4/§16.5, with the [metric registration request](P8.6-integrity-metrics-handover.md).
FEAT-OPS-004 owns rule/panel provisioning, on-call routing and exercised alert
evidence. Audit supplies signal semantics and incident constraints. No rule,
dashboard, routing test or recipient acknowledgement is claimed here.

## Four primary alerts

| Alert | Exact condition | Severity | First action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audit emit failure | Any audit emission failure | P1 | Treat SC-008 attribution as at risk; preserve failure context and confirm the business transaction failed. Never enable unaudited fallback. |
| Audit chain broken | Verification/signature/root-reproduction failure; fork counter >0; root_seq gap or two seals at one position | P1 | Preserve evidence and escalate possible tampering. Never repair the chain or reconcile a fork into a preferred branch. |
| Epoch-seal CAS starvation | Retry rate sustained above 5/min for one tenant, or a single seal retries more than 10 times | P2 | Check canonical (period, retention_class) close ordering. Brief CAS losses are expected; sustained starvation leaves epochs unsealed and blocks disposition. |
| Audit chain-head contention | Lock-wait p95 >50 ms over 10 minutes | P2 | Inspect shard skew, assignment and tenant N before answer latency breaches. Do not change N inside an epoch. |

## Adjacent required alerts

| Alert | Exact condition | Severity | First action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audit checkpoint overdue | audit_checkpoint_overdue_count >0 for 30 minutes | P2 | Inspect the KMS signing path and growing unverified tail; do not dispose unsealed evidence. |
| Audit disposition verification failed | Any audit_epoch_disposition_total{outcome="VERIFY_FAILED"} increment | P1 | Preserve evidence, stop disposition and investigate integrity; do not treat it as an ordinary retryable job error or detach unverifiable evidence. |
| Audit partition held | HOLD_SUSPENDED partition older than 90 days | P3 | Compliance reviews whether the hold is still required; no automatic release or clock reset. |

The architecture does not specify a duration for the word "sustained" in the
CAS alert. Operations must resolve that window with audit ownership and retain
the decision; the 5/min and >10 thresholds must not be weakened. Per-tenant
starvation must remain detectable without adding tenant labels to every series.

## Dashboard 7 panels and acceptance

Provide chain verification history and freshness; sealed/disposed/held epoch
counts and ages; lock-wait p95/p99 and shard-skew trends; disposition/suppression
outcomes and legal-hold review age. Link investigations through protected
evidence references, not secrets or raw payloads. Missing/stale collectors must
show unknown status rather than healthy zero. Retained root-chain evidence must
support drilldown for gaps/forks without exposing cross-tenant records.

Launch acceptance requires the P9 metric adapters, controlled exercises of
every condition, correct P1/P2/P3 routing, panel data/freshness evidence and
first-action/runbook links under FEAT-OPS-004/L5. P9.5/P9.6 and later runbook
tasks remain separate work; this P8.6 handover does not mark them complete.
