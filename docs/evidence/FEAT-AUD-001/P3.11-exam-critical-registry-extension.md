# P3.11 Exam-Critical Registry Extension

Date: 2026-10-07
Feature: `FEAT-AUD-001`

`audit.audit_event` was already registered by `FEAT-PLAT-005 P4.4`. This
change registers every audit proof anchor so CI stage 12 applies the strict
exam-path migration policy to the complete append and verification chain.

| Relation | Why exam-critical | Migration constraints |
|---|---|---|
| `audit.audit_chain_head` | Locked and advanced in every audited business transaction | Expand/contract only; 250 ms exam-critical lock threshold; no blocking alter, rename, or early drop |
| `audit.audit_chain_checkpoint` | Preserves signed prefixes used by sealing and verification | Append-only expansion; no update/delete migration; indexes concurrent after the creation release |
| `audit.audit_chain_root_head` | Serializes the single tenant root chain | Expand/contract only; compare-and-swap columns cannot be rewritten or changed incompatibly |
| `audit.audit_chain_seal` | Permanent proof after event partition disposition | Append-only expansion; no destructive or blocking change; existing signed material is never rewritten |

All five audit relations are schema-qualified in
`migration/exam-critical-tables.yaml`. Removing one weakens the release gate
and requires Platform Ops and Engineering Lead approval under the registry
contract.
