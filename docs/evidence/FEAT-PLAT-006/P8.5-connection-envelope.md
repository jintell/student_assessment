## Runtime Allocation

`cbt-worker` retains its approved ten-connection budget. Runtime-role support
partitions the six background connections into two dedicated lock connections
and four connections for the relay, sweepers, and dispatcher. The four grading
connections remain isolated:

```text
4 grading + 2 sweep-lock + 4 remaining background = 10 worker connections
```

The lock pool permits one serialized sweep and one independent outbox relay.
It is not appended to the worker pool, creates no new database role, and cannot
increase the per-replica allocation beyond ten.
