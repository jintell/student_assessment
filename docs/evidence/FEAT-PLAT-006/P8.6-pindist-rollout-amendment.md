# P8.6 Proposed `cbt-pindist` Rollout Step

Date: 2026-10-16

Status: amendment proposed to the Architecture Owner and `FEAT-OPS-007`.
Runtime roles do not own deployment orchestration.

## Gap

Architecture section 17.6 updates `cbt-api` at step 6 and `cbt-worker` at step
7, but never rolls out the third workload produced by the same image. Omitting
`cbt-pindist` permits an old PIN-distribution runtime to remain deployed after
the shared image, schema, and other workloads advance.

## Proposed Step 6b

Insert the following after the successful `cbt-api` rollout and before the
worker rollout:

```text
6b rolling update of cbt-pindist (maxUnavailable 0, maxSurge 1); require both
   replicas ready on the new immutable digest, the reserved route smoke green,
   the dedicated workload identity unchanged, and every other ingress path 404
```

This position keeps the request-serving canary as the first production
exposure of the new image, then updates the security-isolated route before
background work advances. The fixed two-replica workload retains availability
during replacement and continues to use the same image digest and SBOM as the
other roles.

## Ownership and Acceptance

`FEAT-OPS-007` must accept or amend the exact rollout mechanics, implement the
pipeline step, and retain live rollout evidence. `FEAT-EXAM-002` and
`FEAT-EXAM-003` remain responsible for the route and KMS grant. This proposal
only closes `TASK-PLAT6-OBS-004` as a raised architecture gap; it does not claim
that the deployment sequence is implemented.
